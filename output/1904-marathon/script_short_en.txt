[HOOK]
Ever taken a shortcut to finish something faster?
In 1904, an Olympic runner took one. In a car.

[MYTH + PROMISE]
You'd think the Olympics were always serious.
This marathon was a circus. And the winner? Even stranger.

[CHAPTER 1 - THE HEAT]
St. Louis. Over ninety degrees. Dusty roads.
And just one water stop. A well, at mile twelve.

[CHAPTER 2 - THE CAR]
At mile nine, Fred Lorz gave up... and climbed into his manager's car.
Eleven miles later, the car broke down. So he jogged the rest.
He crossed the line first. The crowd cheered. Then someone spoke up.

[CHAPTER 3 - THE OTHERS]
A Cuban runner stopped to eat apples. They were rotten. He took a nap. He still finished fourth.
A runner from South Africa was chased a mile off course by a dog.

[CHAPTER 4 - THE REAL WINNER]
And the real winner, Thomas Hicks?
His trainers kept him going with egg whites, brandy... and strychnine. Rat poison.
He crossed the line barely standing, his trainers holding him up.

[ENDING]
Thirty-two men started. Only fourteen finished.
One cheated with a car. One survived on poison.
So tell me: which shortcut was worse?
