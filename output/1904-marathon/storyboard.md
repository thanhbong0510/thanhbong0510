# Shorts: Marathon Olympic 1904 — Storyboard 12 cảnh

Định dạng: dọc 9:16, ~60–70 giây, mỗi cảnh ~5 giây.
Phong cách: người que đầu tròn trắng trên nền tranh minh họa sách truyện màu nước (giống video "Lụt bia London").

## Phong cách chung (dán vào ĐẦU mọi prompt ảnh)
```
Vertical 9:16 hand-drawn storybook cartoon illustration, warm watercolor and ink linework, soft muted earthy palette, cream paper border with rounded corners. Characters are simple stick figures with big round white heads, small black dot eyes, very expressive cartoon faces, thin black stick bodies. Highly detailed illustrated background. No text, no letters.
```

## Nhân vật (giữ y nguyên mô tả mỗi lần xuất hiện)
- **Fred Lorz** (kẻ đi ô tô): brown hair tuft, white tank top, white shorts
- **Thomas Hicks** (người thắng): thin black mustache, white tank top with a red stripe
- **Quản lý của Lorz**: bowler hat, driving goggles
- **Carvajal** (người Cuba): long-sleeved white shirt, roughly cut-off trousers, beret

## Prompt ảnh từng cảnh (ghép sau "Phong cách chung")

1. Scene: a modern city park, a stick figure office worker in a grey hoodie with a coffee cup sneakily cutting across the grass instead of following the long curved path, smirking at the viewer.
2. Scene: a dusty 1904 country road, a stick figure runner (brown hair tuft, white tank top, white shorts) relaxing in the back seat of an early 1900s open-top automobile, grinning and waving, exhausted runners struggling in the dust behind.
3. Scene: chaotic start of a 1904 marathon in a dirt stadium, stick figure runners in mismatched old-fashioned outfits, one in long trousers, an official in a bowler hat waving a starting flag, a stray dog running across, spectators in straw boater hats cheering from wooden stands, circus-like mood.
4. Scene: blazing sun over a dusty 1904 dirt road, heat shimmer, exhausted runners crowding around a single old stone well with a wooden bucket, tongues out.
5. Scene: dusty 1904 roadside, the exhausted runner (brown hair tuft, white tank top) climbing happily into an early open-top automobile; the manager (bowler hat, driving goggles) at the wheel beckons him in.
6. Scene: the early automobile broken down with steam pouring from the engine, the manager scratching his head, the runner (brown hair tuft, white tank top) jogging away fresh and cheerful toward a distant city.
7. Scene: 1904 stadium finish line, the runner (brown hair tuft, white tank top) raising both arms in triumph, crowd throwing straw boater hats, an angry spectator in the foreground pointing at him suspiciously.
8. Scene: a small runner (long-sleeved white shirt, cut-off trousers, beret) asleep under an apple tree beside a country road, half-eaten apple in hand, face slightly green.
9. Scene: a panicked runner in a white tank top sprinting through a tall cornfield, chased by a big barking farm dog, corn leaves flying, race road far behind.
10. Scene: a pale exhausted runner (thin black mustache, white tank top with red stripe) slumping on a dusty road while two trainers in bowler hats give him a spoon from a small dark bottle, a cracked egg and a brandy bottle. Comedic mood.
11. Scene: stadium finish line, the dizzy runner (thin black mustache, white tank top with red stripe) with spiral eyes and wobbly legs, held upright under both arms by two trainers in bowler hats, surprised crowd.
12. Scene: split composition on a dusty road: left, the grinning runner (brown hair tuft) lounging in an early automobile; right, the dizzy runner (thin black mustache, red stripe) holding a small dark bottle; middle, a narrator stick figure shrugging at the viewer.

## Prompt chuyển động (Kling / Hailuo — Image to Video, 5 giây)

1. The stick figure walks across the grass taking a shortcut, glances at the camera with a sly smile, leaves sway gently.
2. The old car drives forward bumping on the dusty road, the runner waves happily, dust clouds rise, runners behind stumble.
3. The official waves the flag, runners burst forward chaotically, the dog dashes across, crowd cheers and waves hats.
4. Heat shimmer ripples, runners push each other to reach the well, one pulls up the bucket, water splashes.
5. The runner stops panting, then hops into the car; the manager waves him in and the car starts moving.
6. Steam bursts from the engine, the car shudders to a stop, the manager scratches his head while the runner jogs away cheerfully.
7. The runner throws his arms up, hats fly in the air, then the spectator in front angrily points at him; slow camera push-in.
8. The runner bites the apple, makes a sick face, then lies back and falls asleep, a leaf falls on his face.
9. The dog barks and chases, the runner sprints in panic through the corn, leaves fly, camera follows.
10. The trainers spoon the liquid into the runner's mouth, his eyes spin, then he jolts upright with wide eyes.
11. The runner wobbles across the finish line, trainers hold him up, his head sways dizzily, crowd gasps.
12. The narrator looks from left to right, shrugs and turns to the camera with a questioning look; slow zoom in.

## Ghi chú kiểm chứng
- Lorz bỏ cuộc ở dặm 9, đi xe khoảng 11 dặm, xe hỏng thì chạy nốt; bị cấm thi đấu suốt đời, sau rút xuống 6 tháng. (Wikipedia – Frederick Lorz)
- Hicks được cho uống strychnine + lòng trắng trứng + brandy; có đoạn phải để huấn luyện viên đỡ. (Wikipedia – 1904 men's marathon)
- Carvajal ăn táo hỏng, ngủ một giấc, về thứ 4. Len Tau bị chó đuổi lệch đường khoảng 1 dặm. Chỉ có 1 điểm nước (giếng ở dặm 12). 32 người xuất phát, 14 người về đích.
- "Trên 90°F" là con số được nhắc phổ biến; nên giữ cách nói chung chung.
